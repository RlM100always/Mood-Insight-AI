# Safety and real-life support

Mood Insight is a self-reflection and wellbeing tool. It is not monitored by a clinician, cannot assess immediate risk, and cannot call emergency services on the user's behalf.

The Today and Privacy screens provide a user-initiated link to [Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com/en), a directory of support options by country. A helpline's hours, eligibility, confidentiality and contact methods can vary; users should check the service's own current page.

The app text says to contact local emergency services or the nearest emergency department if someone may be in immediate danger or cannot keep themselves safe. It deliberately avoids hard-coding one emergency number because users may live in different countries.

Activities are optional self-help ideas, not treatment. Users can stop any activity, skip breathing-focused exercises if uncomfortable, and choose a trusted person or qualified professional instead.
