# Australian wellbeing dataset access request — draft

**Do not send until you have filled in the bracketed details and confirmed the intended use.** This draft does not constitute an application, ethics approval or data licence.

**Subject:** Request for data access and reuse conditions — longitudinal mood, sleep and wellbeing research

Dear Data Custodian / Research Team,

I am developing Mood Insight AI, a privacy-focused mobile wellbeing application. I am exploring whether an approved Australian longitudinal dataset could support a carefully evaluated, non-diagnostic research model for next-day mood patterns.

I found the following catalogue record:

- Young and Well Towns Randomised Controlled Trial Data (Flinders University): https://researchdata.edu.au/young-well-towns-trial-data/1793376
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.4226/86/5927896772837

Before applying or using any data, could you please clarify the following?

1. Whether de-identified individual-level records and the codebook/data-completeness documentation are currently available, and the process, fees (if any), eligibility and expected timeframe for access.
2. The actual observation cadence and timestamps for mood, sleep, rest and energy. In particular, whether enough participant-level consecutive-day observations exist to evaluate a next-day target, or whether observations are primarily weekly/ad hoc.
3. The exact variable names, scales, missing-value codes, participant counts and data-cleaning procedures, including whether participant IDs and dates can be used for a secure participant-separated evaluation.
4. The applicable licence. The catalogue page appears to show both a CC BY 4.0 URL and a “Non-commercial Reuse Only/CC BY-NC” label. Please confirm the controlling terms in writing.
5. Whether the licence and participant consent permit machine-learning research, training derived model parameters, publication of aggregate evaluation results, and inclusion/distribution of derived parameters in a public mobile application. If commercial use is excluded, please state what uses are permitted and whether separate written permission could be considered.
6. Required ethics approval, data-use agreement, secure computing environment, retention/deletion requirements, acknowledgements and restrictions on sharing derived data or model artefacts.

**Proposed safeguards:** raw participant records would remain in the approved secure environment and would not be uploaded to GitHub, an app, a general-purpose AI service or a public CI runner. I would use pseudonymous participant IDs, participant-separated evaluation, a chronological test where supported, simple baselines, class-balance and missingness reports, calibration and subgroup/error analysis. I would not claim clinical validity or deploy a model without independent review and any required approvals.
